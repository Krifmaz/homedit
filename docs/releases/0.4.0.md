## homedit 0.4.0

Find services is much faster and finds more.

- **Starts with your own networks.** The targets box now lists the networks the server is really on (each one's /24, Docker networks marked) instead of 192.168.1.0/24, and ranges skip the server's own addresses.
- **Docker apps first.** With a Docker address set, the ports your containers publish show up at once, named after the container, before the network scan starts. Hosts on Docker networks are named after their container.
- **Only live hosts are scanned.** Each address gets a quick check (a few common ports, reverse DNS, the ARP table) and only machines that answer get a full port scan. A whole /24 usually takes well under 30 seconds.
- **Live progress.** While a scan runs you see how many hosts and apps it has found and the apps by name, not just a percentage.
- **Better names.** Each open port is identified as soon as it is found (page title, Server header, then the app usually on that port), and hosts show their DNS or container name.
- **Faster defaults:** 512 connections at once, a 400 ms timeout and no per-second limit (still available in Settings).
- Typing a bare network address such as 10.0.1.0 still offers to scan its /24.
